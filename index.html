The System

Your mind and body are constantly generating information.

Your heart rate changes. Your HRV changes. Your sleep changes. Your activity changes. Your mood changes. Your thoughts, reactions and behavioural patterns change too.

Most wellbeing platforms look at only one part of this picture. STEO brings these signals together. By combining psychological self-reflection, behavioural data, AI pattern recognition and physiological information, STEO helps you understand not only what you are experiencing — but what may be influencing it.

How STEO Works

One system. Multiple layers of understanding.

01

Sense — understand your physical state

STEO can integrate physiological and behavioural signals: heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, activity and daily mood. These signals provide context for how your body is responding across the day.

02

Reflect — understand your internal experience

Through daily challenges, psychological questions, mood check-ins and conversations with STEO, you build a record of emotions, thoughts, reactions, behaviours, recurring situations and personal patterns.

03

Connect — AI looks for relationships across your data

STEO's intelligence layer connects information across time rather than treating each interaction as an isolated moment. It can identify recurring themes and relationships between state, thoughts, behaviour and outcomes.

04

Adapt — turn insight into action

STEO uses what it learns to provide increasingly relevant prompts, practices and challenges — shaped by the patterns it has already seen in you.

Wearables

Your body is part of the conversation.

How you feel psychologically and how your body responds are deeply connected. STEO brings physiological data into the self-awareness process, adding another layer of information beyond what you consciously report.

Heart rate

Heart rate changes dynamically in response to movement, recovery, emotional activation and numerous physiological processes.

Heart rate variability

HRV reflects variation in the timing between heartbeats and can provide useful information about autonomic regulation, recovery and physiological load.

Sleep

Sleep duration and quality influence energy, cognition, emotional regulation and recovery.

Activity

Movement provides additional context for energy expenditure, recovery and changes in daily physiological state.

Context

Data becomes more useful when it has context.

A heart-rate number by itself tells you very little about your life. The same is true for HRV, sleep or activity. STEO is designed to interpret these signals alongside your reported experience.

Physiology
  • Reduced sleep
  • Changed HRV
  • Elevated resting heart rate
Self-report
  • Low energy
  • High emotional activation
  • Poor concentration

  • Work pressure

The STEO Loop

The pattern behind behaviour.

Every experience creates another input into what happens next.

Impulse

Something happens.

State

Your physiological and emotional state changes.

Interpretation

Your mind gives the experience meaning.

Choice

You respond based on that interpretation.

Action

The choice becomes behaviour.

Outcome

The behaviour creates a result.

New state

The result changes your next internal state, and the loop begins again.

STEO helps you see the loop while it is happening.

Because changing the outcome often begins much earlier than the outcome itself.
